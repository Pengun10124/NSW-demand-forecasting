NSW1 Minimum Load Inputs

Prepared 19 October 2026. English model inputs derived from our existing three core study tables. Original files remain unchanged.

Choose the correct data

The workbook contains ML Training Data (2025-01-01 to 2026-08-31, 608 days) and Baseline 2023 (365 days). Matching CSVs are included. The two later source periods overlap: 5,856 duplicate half-hours were removed. The 2023 period is kept separate because there is no 2024 data in our core inputs. Do not concatenate the sheets and then use positional shift(1) or shift(7) across the gap.

The main sheet passes the notebook's complete-day and continuous-half-hour checks. It contains 29,184 rows. All 17,520 rows of 2023 are retained separately. This package does not reproduce the other team's 2024-07 to 2026-06 experiment.

What changed

Removed electricity price, rooftop PV, humidity, weather descriptions and the predispatch sequence code because this notebook does not read them. Dates and clock time remain in the full Interval_Start_AEST timestamp. Daily targets, seasonal features, day length, temperature summaries and lag features are computed by the notebook and are not duplicated in this input file.

Temperature was recovered from the already-downloaded IEM METAR archive for Sydney Airport (YSSY). Conversion: (tmpf − 32) × 5/9. Only observations exactly on the half-hour endpoint were joined. Off-grid reports were not shifted into slots. Missing codes M remain blank; temperatures were not interpolated or forward-filled. Wind values retain the earlier four-station mean and forward-fill processing. Temperature and wind are endpoint observations, not interval averages. The public-holiday flags follow the notebook's fixed-AEST interval-start calendar convention rather than converting to Sydney daylight-saving time.

All demand values were preserved. Duplicate demand values agree. One overlapping wind value differs at 2026-01-01 00:00 AEST: the 2025 source carried the previous reading (4.115525 m/s), while the recent source has four-station observations (3.472500 m/s). The recent value was retained. This is a source-boundary correction, not outlier removal.

Missing data and limitations

Temperature is blank in 332 main-sheet rows and 566 baseline rows. No new values were removed as outliers. Observed temperatures range from 6 to 42 °C in the main sheet and 6 to 43 °C in 2023; high temperature alone was not used as a deletion rule. Demand and wind have no missing values, but some wind readings are inherited forward fills. See quality_and_provenance.csv for station counts, fill flags, observation age and source period.

The notebook aggregates temperature over available readings each day, so an incomplete day can miss the true daily temperature minimum or maximum. Its model pipeline handles missing daily predictors, but does not repair incomplete daily extrema. These are observed weather inputs: they are unavailable at midnight in a real forecast. Do not describe the evaluation as using operational weather forecasts.

The holiday flag excludes local-only holidays and the bank-only August closure. It includes the additional statewide Anzac holiday on 27 April 2026, verified against the current NSW government calendar.
