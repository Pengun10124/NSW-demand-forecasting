Combined data: original rows take priority

Both original workbooks and notebooks remain unchanged. The new CSV combines our 2023 and later main data with the original workbook. Matching interval-start timestamps retain the entire original row, including its missing values. No cross-source imputation was applied.

Total: 55,536 rows (1,157 complete days). Removed 26,208 overlapping rows from our inputs. Missing January–June 2024 remains a gap. Calendar lags reset at each continuous segment; 14 warm-up days are excluded.

The model uses 990 training days and the same 91 test days, April–June 2026, as the original-data comparison. All test demand targets match the original data exactly. July–August 2026 is included in the merged file but excluded from this run to avoid training on dates after the test. The original model grids and seed 42 are unchanged. Model selection uses training-period time-series CV.

Interpretation

  • The combined run extends the original training data with 2023. Under this cutoff, our July–August 2026 additions are not used.
  • Wind has mixed definitions: original weighted regional wind on original timestamps, and our available-station mean elsewhere. Temperature observation processing also differs. The provenance CSV records the source for every row.
  • The low-load threshold is recomputed from each training set, so classification labels and scores are not directly comparable across datasets without a common fixed threshold.
  • This is an additional experiment on a previously inspected test period, not a fresh untouched final validation. Keep a new hold-out for final claims. Upstream CV selection and full-training quantile definitions are retained, not nested validation.
  • Observed weather is a perfect-weather assumption. Results do not represent operational forecast performance or an official grid safety limit.
