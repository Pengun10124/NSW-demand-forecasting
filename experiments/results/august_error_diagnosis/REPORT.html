August 2026 error diagnosis

Conclusion: Evidence points primarily to limited model responsiveness under more volatile daily load, rather than a demand merge or averaging error. This is a diagnosis of local archived inputs, not independent verification of all upstream observations.

Data reconciliation

All 2,976 July-August half-hours match the means of exactly six five-minute readings in the local AEMO PRICE_AND_DEMAND_202607_NSW1.csv and PRICE_AND_DEMAND_202608_NSW1.csv files. Maximum absolute difference is 0.000000334 MW (rounding). The source has unique timestamps. Interval endpoints are shifted back 30 minutes to match the model's interval-start convention. No demand gaps or duplicate model timestamps were found.

July temperature has 12 missing half-hours; August has 16 out of 1,488 (1.08%). There are zero and two inherited wind forward fills respectively. The largest error, August 9, has no missing temperature or filled wind. These checks do not show missing data as the main explanation. Available-reading daily summaries may still be imperfect.

Judge improvements on independent future dates. Do not delete large-error days: source reconciliation gives no basis to classify these demand observations as corrupt.
