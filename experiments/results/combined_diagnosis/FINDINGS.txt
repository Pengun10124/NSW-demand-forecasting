Combined-data diagnostic

Same April-June 2026 targets, same preprocessing and fixed model parameters.
Ridge: original history MAE 316.312 MW; combined history 301.035 MW.
Combined-selected Gradient Boosting (identical parameters): original history 368.306 MW; combined history 344.453 MW.
Thus both fixed models improved after adding 2023. The reported regression was a change in selected model, not deterioration of a fixed model.

Adding historical rows also changed the five TimeSeriesSplit validation windows. Combined CV narrowly selected GB (468.365) over Ridge (475.539) on its own windows. Using the original five validation date windows for both histories, combined-history Ridge scored 433.269 MW versus 458.604 MW for the combined-selected GB configuration. This is a two-model diagnostic, not a new exhaustive tuning run.

Ridge wind ablation: original 317.056 MW without wind; combined 300.805 MW without wind. This gives no evidence that wind definition mixing is the main cause of the reported MAE increase for Ridge; it does not establish that the mixed source is harmless in general.

Combined-selected GB underpredicts June minima by 204.71 MW on average; the original Ridge bias is -103.33 MW. Inspect monthly_errors.csv and daily_errors.csv for details.

These are exploratory diagnostics on an already inspected hold-out. Do not report the 301.035 score as fresh untouched confirmation or select future models solely from this test. Model and original data files were not changed.
