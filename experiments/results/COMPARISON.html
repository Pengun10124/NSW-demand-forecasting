NSW dataset trials

Three datasets were run through the upstream model definitions, tuning grids and seed 42. Both original source files and the original notebook are preserved. These are retrospective experiments with observed weather.

How to compare

  • The original and our main data use the same April–June 2026 hold-out (91 days), but the original training starts in July 2024 and ours in January 2025. Weather sources and demand preparation also differ. Differences in errors do not isolate data quality.
  • The 2023 baseline uses October–December 2023 (92 days). Its season, year and training history differ: treat it as a separate period trial, not a direct ranking.
  • The additional interactive notebook still defaults to June–August 2026. This comparison script explicitly uses April–June for the overlapping period.
  • No models were retuned after reviewing hold-out scores. Upstream CV-based model selection and full-training 10% risk thresholds are retained; this is not nested validation. A statistical low-load label is not an official grid safety threshold.
  • When no low-load positives occur, recall/F1 returned as zero are conventions and ROC-AUC is undefined. Inspect positive counts before interpreting classification.
